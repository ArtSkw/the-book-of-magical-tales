REFERENCE ROLES: the Pentiment references define the drawing style, line and level of simplification — follow them closely. The TONE REFERENCES (two finished pages of this book: the kneeling scene on the bridge and the helpers walking onto the bridge) define the colour balance and warmth every picture must share. The character sheets define identity, costume and colours of each character only (re-draw them in the Pentiment manner). The location sheet defines ONLY the shape and layout of the place — the bridge, the nest crack, the banks, the buildings — which must match it exactly; ignore its colours, they are washed out. Never copy a composition from a reference.
